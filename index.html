About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at Grand Valley State University and an IT Field Service Technician supporting 8 Lowe's sites across West Michigan. Each site runs around 60 endpoints, 8 servers and ~150 rotating users per day. I handle everything from retail POS systems, printer and cash recycler repair, to network troubleshooting on enterprise incident tickets.

Before that, I was an IT intern at the Grand Rapids Housing Commission, where I deployed custom desktop images, configured client and site-to-site VPNs, supported Windows and Linux machines, and wrote technical documentation.

Fixing production systems every day is what made me want to build them. I like building software end to end: Python, FastAPI, Docker, PostgreSQL, Redis, and the monitoring that keeps it honest with Prometheus and Grafana. Outside of code, I rebuild motorcycle engines and restore vintage computers and watches. Same instinct, different hardware.
