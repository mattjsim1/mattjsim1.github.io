About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at Grand Valley State University and an IT Field Service Technician with CompuCom, supporting 8 Lowe's locations across West Michigan. Each site runs around 60 endpoints and 8 servers, and I handle everything from retail POS systems and printer hardware to network troubleshooting on enterprise incident tickets.

Before that, I was an IT intern at the Grand Rapids Housing Commission, where I deployed custom desktop images, configured client and site-to-site VPNs, supported Windows and Linux machines, and wrote technical documentation.

Fixing production systems every day is what made me want to build them. I'm focused on backend and DevOps engineering: Python, FastAPI, Docker, PostgreSQL, Redis, and monitoring with Prometheus and Grafana. Outside of code, I rebuild motorcycle engines and restore vintage computers and watches. Same instinct, different hardware.
